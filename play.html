Quick reference

Actions explained

Fold

Give up your hand and any chips in the pot. Use when your hand is weak.

Check

Pass action without betting. Only available if no one has bet this round.

Call

Match the current bet to stay in the hand. You see the next card (or showdown).

Raise

Increase the bet. Forces opponents to pay more or fold. Shows strength (or bluffs it).
